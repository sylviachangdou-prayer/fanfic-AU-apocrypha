# Wizarding-World and Magic-School AU Guide

Use this guide for Harry Potter, Fantastic Beasts, Cursed Child, official companion material, or an original magic-school AU. Read [source-grounding.md](source-grounding.md) first for a named canon.

## 1. Select continuity

Record which layers apply:

- Seven-book continuity and exact cutoff.
- Eight-film adaptation and accepted film-only changes.
- Fantastic Beasts film continuity and historical period.
- Cursed Child stage continuity.
- Official companion writing formerly associated with Pottermore and now available through official archives.
- User-selected fanon.

Do not create an unspecified blend. Fix character ages, school year, current Minister, war status, living and dead characters, known secrets, and whether the epilogue applies.

## 2. Preserve the source's story engine

The setting works through collisions among:

- Intimate school life and lethal political history.
- Improvised adolescent action and slow adult institutions.
- Whimsical material culture and punitive law.
- Family inheritance and chosen loyalty.
- Incomplete education, unequal preparation, and dangerous knowledge.
- Rumor, newspapers, portraits, letters, and official records as competing information systems.

An AU should preserve at least two of these tensions even when Hogwarts, wands, or the British Ministry are replaced.

## 3. Institutional architecture

Map the relevant offices and their dependencies rather than using "the Ministry" as one actor.

### Government and law

- Minister and senior administration: political responsibility and appointment power.
- Magical Law Enforcement: investigation, prosecution, field response, and internal politics are distinct functions even when the canon leaves boundaries vague.
- Magical Accidents and Catastrophes: concealment, memory modification, and disaster response.
- Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures: classification conflicts, licensing, prejudice, and interest groups.
- International Magical Cooperation: treaty obligations and foreign jurisdiction.
- Magical Transportation: networks, permissions, accidents, and monitoring.
- Magical Games and Sports: league administration and mass events.
- Department of Mysteries: knowledge controlled through secrecy and restricted access.
- Wizengamot, trials, imprisonment, and enforcement: verify procedure at the chosen date rather than inventing a modern court system.

### Semi-independent powers

- Hogwarts: headship, staff, houses, prefects, governors, admissions, discipline, examinations, and informal student power.
- Gringotts: goblin-run banking, custody, inheritance, security, and a political relationship with wizard government; never treat it as a generic Ministry office.
- St Mungo's: diagnosis, magical injury categories, records, staffing, and privacy.
- Daily Prophet and other media: access, editorial pressure, reputation, and rumor.
- Families, shops, professional guilds, Quidditch organizations, and international bodies.

For an original AU, build equivalents with competing jurisdiction. A school, bank, hospital, press, and government should not share one chain of command unless the premise explains why.

## 4. Magic as an operating system

Do not impose a generic mana bar unless the AU explicitly adds one. Track the relevant mechanism:

| Domain | Questions |
|---|---|
| Wand magic | Incantation, motion, intent, skill, range, aim, counter, trace |
| Potions | Ingredients, preparation, time, contamination, storage, dosage, antidote |
| Transfiguration and charms | Duration, maintenance, reversal, mass, identity, failure residue |
| Curses and healing | Damage mechanism, treatment window, permanent effects, legal evidence |
| Enchanted objects | Maker, ownership, activation, limits, maintenance, audit trail |
| Divination and prophecy | Interpretation, access, self-fulfillment, institutional custody |
| Creatures and plants | Agency, ecology, handling, trade, legal classification |
| Transport | Access point, destination knowledge, monitoring, capacity, bodily cost |
| Detection and concealment | What is detected, by whom, at what resolution, and with which false positives |

Magic solves one layer while creating another. Instant transport changes commuting, policing, smuggling, and class access. Memory alteration changes evidence and trust. Transformation changes identity verification. Enchanted labor changes household politics.

## 5. School as a lived institution

Use:

- Timetables, homework, notes, practical rooms, office hours, clubs, punishment, exams, and unequal prior tutoring.
- Dormitory privacy, bathing, laundry, illness, food, mail, holidays, money, travel, and family contact.
- Teachers with workloads, specialties, grudges, professional ties, and incomplete knowledge.
- House affiliation as an incentive network, not a complete personality test.
- Student rumor as a fast but distorted intelligence service.
- Curriculum gaps that explain dangerous improvisation without making every adult absent or foolish.

In an adult-school AU, replace parental authority with employment schedules, licensing, tuition, childcare, housing, labor status, and professional accreditation.

## 6. Social structure and ordinary life

Track blood prejudice, Muggle-born access, Squib status, goblin and house-elf relations, werewolf restrictions, old-family capital, war affiliation, geographic isolation, and international difference only when they affect characters.

Make prejudice operational: hiring, marriage strategy, inheritance, school discipline, medical care, testimony, housing, press framing, access to training, and who is presumed credible.

Use material anchors: robes and repair, owl care, Floo soot, cauldrons, parchment supply, ingredient freshness, wand maintenance, small household spells, queues, Ministry lifts, hospital forms, pub gossip, and the inconvenience of secrecy around non-magical relatives.

## 7. Character-anchor method

Derive anchors from repeated source decisions, not fandom labels. For each character record:

1. What they do before they understand the full danger.
2. Which kind of unfairness or shame breaks their strategy.
3. How they ask for help, if at all.
4. How fear changes their cruelty, silence, humor, or competence.
5. Which relationships override abstract principles.

Common calibration risks:

- Do not make Harry a passive saint, omniscient veteran, or generic action hero. Preserve his action bias, suspicion of authority, selective recklessness, dry judgment, and attachment patterns as applicable to the chosen age.
- Do not make Hermione an infallible research engine. Preserve preparation, procedural intelligence, moral insistence, social blind spots, impatience, and the moments when injustice defeats strategy.
- Do not make Draco a generic elegant executive. Preserve status awareness, performance, family pressure, risk sensitivity, verbal aggression, cowardice and courage in their specific contexts, and his capacity to misjudge power.

User-supplied anchors override these broad cautions.

## 8. AU translation

Translate dramatic function:

- Auror function may become investigator, sentinel, operative, regulator, or licensed hunter, but keep the character's relationship to authority and danger.
- House or family status may become corporate tier, military lineage, coven ancestry, noble house, citizenship class, or professional network.
- Magic may become technology, psychic ability, ritual, bureaucratic privilege, or scarce expertise only if its access and social meaning remain consequential.
- Hogwarts may become academy, hospital training program, night school, agency pipeline, monastery, or orbital institute. Rebuild schedule, discipline, credentials, peer groups, and hidden curriculum.

Do not translate every noun one-to-one. Some source institutions should disappear, leaving characters without familiar protection.

## 9. Fanon control

Treat the following as fanon or explicit AU devices unless verified for the selected canon: magical cores, universal soul bonds, inherited lordship stacks, automatic political seats, Gringotts ancestry tests, painless emancipation, formal marriage laws, ritualized "light" and "dark" political blocs, wards as an all-purpose system, and personality determined by Hogwarts house.

Fanon may be used deliberately. Give it an origin, beneficiaries, enforcement mechanism, limits, dissenters, and costs. Do not import it unconsciously and call it canon.

## 10. Set pieces and audit

Strong setting-specific set pieces include a mass magical event, examination failure with public stakes, moving-building crisis, hospital quarantine, Ministry hearing, contested trial, prison or archive breach, school siege, transport cascade, creature incident, or ritual whose legal meaning changes mid-scene.

Audit:

1. Does magic create a visible procedure and failure mode?
2. Are school, government, bank, hospital, media, and family powers distinguishable?
3. Is every canon claim assigned to the selected continuity?
4. Do character choices remain recognizable after the AU translation?
5. Does an iconic element affect logistics or relationships rather than appear as decoration?
6. Does the large scene change evidence, authority, access, or allegiance afterward?
