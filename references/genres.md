## 2. Apocalypse and zombies

### Select the collapse stage

- **Onset**: contradictory alerts, institutional triage, transport choke points, family separation, and incomplete threat knowledge.
- **Fragmentation**: utilities fail unevenly, jurisdictions conflict, supply chains break, and records determine access.
- **Survival network**: routes, settlements, markets, quarantine, repair, diplomacy, and memory of failed authority.
- **Second generation**: people inherit rules and stories they did not witness; old infrastructure becomes custom or taboo.

### Threat contract

Define transmission, incubation, symptoms, sensory attraction, temperature effects, decomposition, mobility, animal vectors, immunity, testing, and what survivors can observe. Distinguish fact, official claim, rumor, and character inference.

Model crowds through sound, light, slope, barriers, bottlenecks, weather, and accumulated bodies. Do not spawn danger wherever pacing slows.

### Survival systems

Track water, calories, cooking fuel, medicine, sanitation, footwear, sleep, childcare, waste, dental care, batteries, tools, weather, and trustworthy information. Every refuge needs intake, storage, labor, rules, and failure points.

### Institutions

Use rosters, wristbands, ration categories, quarantine decisions, burial crews, checkpoint jurisdiction, missing-person records, medical custody, property claims, convoy schedules, and appeals. Officials, soldiers, clinicians, volunteers, traders, and families should have competing incentives.

### Avoid

Instant universal collapse, armies that vanish solely to empower protagonists, endless ammunition, identical cruel factions, infection as a surprise without a rule trail, and brutality as the only realism.

## 3. Wasteland and post-collapse societies

### Ecology and settlement

Map water catchments, arable soil, contamination, wind, elevation, seasonal temperature, grazing, forests, ruins, roads, mines, and waste. Avoid an endless sand plain unless geology and climate justify it.

Define how a settlement obtains food, water, heat, materials, medicine, labor, defense, and new members. Determine what it exports and what dependency can break it.

### Knowledge and repair

Mechanical knowledge is a scarce resource. Track compatible parts, measurement standards, lubricants, tools, apprenticeships, manuals, oral shortcuts, and machines cannibalized to keep another alive. Expertise should contain gaps and local traditions.

### Law and organizations

Use water courts, copied land deeds, archive keepers, seed registries, repair guilds, caravan guarantees, debt tallies, salvage claims, shrine custody, militias, and hostages. Legitimacy may belong to whoever holds records, keys, working pumps, or remembered procedure.

Give raiders, traders, farmers, nomads, engineers, and religious communities economic reasons for their behavior. A visual subculture is not an organization.

### Story pressure

Let a repaired system redistribute power. A new well, route, radio, seed stock, or archive should create winners, losers, claims, sabotage, and unwanted migration.

### Avoid

Scrap fetish without function, punk fashion as personality, radiation as convenient magic, empty ruins without former use, and self-sufficient settlements with no labor or waste.

## 4. Medieval societies

### Political economy

Track grain, fodder, salt, iron, timber, mills, roads, tolls, markets, coin shortage, rents, labor dues, winter storage, and control of armed retainers. Power depends on households and local enforcement more than abstract borders.

### Law and jurisdiction

Map crown, lord, manor, town, guild, church, household, and customary authority. Different courts may claim the same person. Use oaths, witnesses, seals, charters, sanctuary, ordeal, compensation, hostage, marriage, inheritance, and reputation according to the invented society rather than generic "medieval law."

### Religion and literacy

Religious institutions may control education, records, marriage, burial, charity, calendar, legitimacy, and sanctuary while containing internal orders and disputes. Literate clerks, notaries, monks, merchants, and administrators possess different knowledge.

### War and movement

Calculate roads, ships, horses, baggage, muster, fodder, harvest, disease, siege time, and winter. A wounded or unpaid force changes local politics. Most people cannot abandon farms indefinitely.

### Ordinary life

Use crop varieties, brewing, wet wool, smoke, candles, animal care, bedding, childcare, tools, measures, local speech, market days, and repair. Avoid a uniformly dirty, ignorant population.

### Avoid

Modern liberal assumptions without institutions or cost, universal standard speech, honor as the only motive, consequence-free female or low-status mobility, and kings who directly administer every village.

## 5. ABO

ABO has no universal canon. Define only the variables relevant to the story.

### Biology contract

Decide distribution, manifestation age, scent production and perception, spatial decay, cycles, blockers, medical side effects, bonding or marking, reversibility, fertility, pregnancy if relevant, inheritance, individual variation, disability, and whether any common belief is false.

Secondary sex cannot determine personality, morality, competence, dominance, sexual role, or relationship preference.

### Institutions

Map registration, health records, education, employment, housing, military service, insurance, marriage, custody, inheritance, criminal law, medical consent, blockers, leave, public accommodation, and identity disclosure. Add black markets, mutual aid, advocacy, professional associations, family strategy, and religious doctrine as relevant.

### Material life

Use ventilation, laundry, dosage schedules, private rooms, clinic language, scent etiquette, counterfeit medicine, travel kits, emergency care, workplace forms, and the gap between public euphemism and bodily experience.

### Relationship pressure

Track who can refuse, who controls medicine or documentation, how scent is interpreted or manipulated, and whether compliance, desire, attachment, survival strategy, and consent are distinct. A biological event may intensify an existing conflict but cannot create characterization by itself.

### Avoid

Universal alpha command, coercion treated as automatic romance, identical bodies and cycles, pregnancy assumed without direction, miraculous bonding that resolves distrust, and a romance isolated from the social system.

## 6. Cyberpunk

### Core system

Cyberpunk concerns ownership and access under networked power: identity, bodies, labor, housing, data, attention, medicine, energy, and the right to disconnect. Neon is optional.

### Network contract

Map physical nodes, power, cooling, bandwidth, latency, authentication, logging, permissions, backups, maintenance, vendor lock-in, and failure recovery. Remote access has a route and leaves evidence. Data's value depends on provenance, timing, exclusivity, and enforceability.

### Body and medicine

Define implant purpose, surgery, healing, rejection, firmware, subscription, calibration, spare parts, pain, accessibility, warranty, repossession, and who may disable service. Bodies are not interchangeable fashion platforms.

### Institutions

Use employers, platforms, landlords, clinics, insurers, identity authorities, private security, contractors, rating systems, content moderation, customer service, debt collectors, unions, mutual aid, repair shops, and black markets. Corporations contain rival divisions, budgets, outsourced layers, and incomplete information.

### Material life

Use charging queues, obsolete connectors, device heat, ad-supported rooms, screen glare, repair scars, inaccessible interfaces, delivery windows, clinic smell, account recovery, subscription expiration, and rare offline silence.

### Avoid

Neon rain as the only atmosphere, instant hacking, corporations as omniscient single minds, technology speeches, data that matters without a buyer or proof, and characters whose only trait is stylish alienation.

## 7. Hybrid settings

Before drafting, answer:

1. Which setting supplies material rules?
2. Which supplies institutions?
3. Which supplies the emotional promise?
4. Which source has priority when rules conflict?
5. What new economy or jurisdiction emerges from the combination?
6. Which familiar image from each setting will be omitted?

Make the hybrid causal. Vampire cyberpunk means blood supply, daylight access, identity renewal, biotechnology, surveillance, and service ownership transform one another. Harry Potter assassin AU means character behavior, magical detection, Ministry authority, cover identity, and mission organizations collide. It is not a costume swap.
